About MediFlow

What is MediFlow?

MediFlow is a web application designed to help ophthalmologists significantly speed up their process of analyzing Optical Coherence Tomographies (OCTs), saving valuable time and reducing errors.

But... Why is it faster?

MediFlow allows ophthalmologists to upload their patients' test results, automatically extracting the patient's information and saving it to our database. Once a patient is created, with just a few clicks, you can view their test result, analyze it, and send it via email.

How is it different from the current process?

The traditional process involves obtaining the exam's PDF file, writing the analysis in a text editor, transcribing the analysis into the patient's clinical record, and then sending the patient an email with the exam result and a copy of their medical record. With MediFlow, the process is streamlined and fully integrated: upload the file, write your analysis, and send the analyzed exam via email with just one click. It's that simple.

Did you know...

The traditional process takes an average of 10 minutes per exam. With MediFlow, the average analysis time is just 2 minutes. That's a 5x increase in productivity!
